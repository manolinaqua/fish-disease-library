# Motile Aeromonas Septicemia

## Etiology
- Pathogen: *Aeromonas hydrophila* and related species

## Clinical Signs
- Hemorrhagic lesions on skin and fins
- Ulceration and necrosis
- Systemic septicemia
- Mortality in severe outbreaks

## Epidemiology
- Opportunistic pathogen
- Associated with:
  - environmental stress
  - poor water quality
  - high organic load

## Diagnosis
- Clinical observation
- Bacterial isolation
- Laboratory confirmation

## Treatment
- Antibiotic therapy (regulated use)
- Water quality improvement

## Prevention
- Sanitation and biosecurity
- Stress minimization
