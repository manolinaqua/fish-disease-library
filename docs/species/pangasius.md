# Pangasianodon hypophthalmus (Pangasius)

## Taxonomy
- Family: Pangasiidae  
- Order: Siluriformes  

## Common Names
- Pangasius
- Tra fish
- Vietnamese catfish

## Distribution
Primarily farmed in Southeast Asia, with major production in the Mekong Delta (Vietnam). Distributed globally through aquaculture exports.

## Production System
- Intensive pond culture
- High stocking density systems
- Freshwater environments with controlled feeding and aeration

## Importance in Aquaculture
Pangasius is one of the most economically significant freshwater fish species worldwide, contributing substantially to global whitefish supply chains.

## Health Considerations
Disease outbreaks are among the most critical constraints, especially in:
- high-density farming systems
- fluctuating environmental conditions
- regions with high pathogen load

## Key Disease Categories
- Bacterial diseases (Edwardsiella, Aeromonas)
- Parasitic infections (protozoa and ectoparasites)
- Fungal infections (Saprolegnia spp.)
- Environmental and gill-related disorders
